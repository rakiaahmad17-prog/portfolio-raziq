  • Tariff for 36 treatments with live search

  • Full Bahasa Indonesia copy, written for patients

04 — About

One designer, the whole way through.

No agency layer, no handoff, no account manager repeating your brief back to you.

I'm Ahmad Raziq Abiyuqail Umarella — a freelance web designer based in Makassar, South Sulawesi.

I build websites for clinics, restaurants and small teams across Indonesia: businesses that are excellent in person and invisible online. Most of them don't need a bigger marketing budget. They need one page that answers the questions their front desk answers forty times a day.

I work alone, which means the person who takes your brief is the person who designs it, builds it, and answers your message three weeks after launch. No telephone game between three departments.

My defaults are boring on purpose: real copy before pretty layouts, 390px before 1440px, and a page that loads on Makassar 4G before it loads on my laptop.

How I work

  1. Copy before pixels. If the words don't make sense, no amount of spacing will fix it.
  2. Design at 390px first. Most of your visitors will never see the layout I fell in love with on a 27-inch screen.
  3. Launch beats perfect. A live site you can improve teaches you more than a prototype you keep polishing.
